# Exercise sheet 1

Exercise 1. (4 pts) (Two coins)
Toss two fair coins. What is the probability of two heads given that at least one coin shows heads?

Exercise 2. (6 pts) (Medical test)
A disease affects 1 percent of people. A test detects it in 99 percent of sick people and gives a false positive in 5 percent of healthy people. What is the probability of being sick after a positive test?

Exercise 3. (Extra)
Prove the law of total probability.
