# Bayes' Theorem

## 1. Conditional probability

The probability of A given B is the probability of both events divided by the probability of B. It is defined when P(B) is positive.

$$ P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)} $$

## 2. Law of total probability

If B1 to Bn split the sample space into disjoint cases, the probability of A is the weighted sum of its conditional probabilities. Each case contributes in proportion to its own probability.

## 3. Bayes' theorem

Bayes' theorem reverses a conditional probability. The prior is updated into the posterior once the data is observed.
